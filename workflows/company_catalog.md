# spejder.workflows.company_catalog

**Purpose:** Orchestrate company catalog merge / rename / delete, plus the shared late-sync empty-company prune stage. Persistence and `resolve_company` live in `spejder.db.companies`; this module does not run SQL.

**Outcome record — Company Avoid flag**

**Problem:**
Operators need a durable way to mark a catalog company as one they do not want to triage, so membership jobs leave active triage via the existing viewed path, without soft-hiding, without changing scores, and without fighting Promote.

**Intent:**
Add a manual company **Avoid** flag that auto-views jobs for that company going forward (and optionally in bulk when the operator confirms), stays independent of Promote, and reuses existing viewed / active-rescore gates instead of a parallel Avoid skip path.

**Scenario:**
An operator opens the main dashboard Companies tab, toggles Avoid on a catalog company (on or off), answers the bulk dialog, and later ingests or enriches jobs for that company; membership jobs already Applied, on Interview, or Stopped keep their pipeline meaning.

**Behavior:**
- Persist Avoid on the company catalog flags bitfield with the same pattern as Promote and Touched (another flag bit; durable across restarts).
- Expose an Avoid toggle on the Companies tab comparable to Promote (checkbox + API toggle that persists the flag). Companies column order left → right: row select, Promote, Avoid, Action, Company, Added, then the existing stats columns (Jobs, Not viewed, Relevant, Not relevant, Applied, Interview, Stopped, Aliases).
- Promote and Avoid are independent. Neither auto-clears the other. The Promote checkbox stays usable when Avoid is on (preserve misclick and the prior Promote value). Promote auto logic stays apply / interview / stop-driven; Avoid is manual only. Jobs already viewed because of Avoid leave triage regardless of Promote.
- Scoring does not apply an Avoid penalty or otherwise change relevance because of Avoid. Early viewed is enough.
- Soft-hide is not the Avoid mechanism. Do not soft-hide membership jobs when Avoid turns on. Leave category, applied, and pipeline flags alone; for existing jobs the only automatic effect of Avoid (aside from the flag itself) is setting viewed when the operator chooses bulk Yes, or when ingest marks viewed for an Avoid company.
- Turning Avoid on or off does not by itself rewrite all membership jobs’ viewed bits. The flag always controls auto-viewing for future ingest / portal / upsert once the company resolves as Avoid. Whenever the operator raises or lowers Avoid, show a dialog with three choices, matching Stop / Promote dialog quality (modal where Companies already uses modals; otherwise the same dashboard dialog style as existing company confirms—not a crude one-button confirm):
  - **Yes** — change Avoid and apply bulk viewed mutations in one transactional story with the flag when possible. Turning Avoid **on**: set viewed on membership jobs that are not already viewed (non-destructive for already-viewed). Turning Avoid **off**: unview **all safe non-pipeline** membership jobs that are currently viewed — safe means not applied, not on interview, and not interview-stopped, using the same pipeline predicates the product already uses elsewhere. Accept that this may unview jobs the operator marked viewed by hand. There is no Avoid-viewed provenance tracking in this iteration. Applied / Interview / Stopped (pipeline) membership jobs **must stay viewed**. Bulk unview on Off+Yes must clear viewed **without** clearing Promote and must not disturb Touched or Promote independence — do not use a path that drops Promote (or Touched) as a side effect of unview.
  - **No** — change only the Avoid flag (and keep future auto-view wired from the new flag value); leave all existing membership viewed bits unchanged.
  - **Misclick / Cancel** — do not change Avoid and do not bulk view or unview.
- When merging companies, the surviving Avoid bit is the **OR** of the participants’ Avoid bits (same pattern as Promote’s OR).
- For new jobs and enrichment: prefer insert-then-mark-viewed (or mark viewed in the same ingest path as soon as the company resolves as Avoid). Do not invent a parallel “skip enrichment if Avoid” rule. Rely on existing viewed and active-rescore-scope gates for skills materialize, missing-description triage, and active rescore. Inbox title / raw translation may still run during ingest; mitigate by marking viewed as soon as practical after upsert / resolve so later sync stages skip. Early viewed is sufficient; there is no parallel Avoid skip path. Only add a narrow Avoid-aware ingest tweak if a clear leftover cost remains that viewed-too-late cannot fix—and if that ever happens, document why here; otherwise early viewed alone is the contract.
- Use-case expectations: Avoid bit persists and toggles via API like Promote; Yes bulk on sets viewed on not-yet-viewed membership jobs; Yes bulk off unviews all currently viewed safe non-pipeline membership jobs without clearing Promote/Touched; Cancel leaves flag and viewed unchanged; No flips only the flag; ingest / portal / upsert auto-views when the company is Avoid; merge keeps Avoid as OR of participants; Companies chrome shows Avoid between Promote and Action; Promote remains independently toggleable; no Avoid score change; no soft-hide-as-Avoid.
- Failure that matters: Avoid soft-hides or mutates category / applied / pipeline; Promote and Avoid become mutually exclusive or Promote is disabled while Avoid is on; toggling Avoid silently rewrites all membership viewed bits without a Yes/No/Cancel choice; Off+Yes unviews Applied / Interview / Stopped; Off+Yes clears Promote or Touched as a side effect of unview; merge drops a participant’s Avoid instead of OR; Avoid changes scores; or a parallel Avoid skip path duplicates what early viewed already gates.
- Non-behavior / not done: soft-hide as the Avoid mechanism; mutually exclusive Promote/Avoid; an Avoid scoring penalty; Avoid-viewed provenance tracking (this iteration unviews all safe non-pipeline viewed membership jobs on Off+Yes).

**API:**
- `run_empty_company_prune(db_path, *, on_stage=None) -> list[int]` — shared late sync stage: optional `on_stage("empty_companies", "Pruning empty companies")`, then `prune_empty_companies(db_path)` (full catalog, `company_ids=None`). Used by GUI sync and CLI `process-inbox` after profile keyword learning. Not part of skill hygiene. Do not call on early skipped empty sync. Merge keeps its own scoped prune of loser ids (unchanged).
- `merge_companies(db_path, company_ids, display_name)` — one DB txn via `merge_company_rows` (`UPDATE company_aliases SET company_id=survivor WHERE company_id=loser` for each loser so **all** participant alias keys move to the survivor — including rename-history keys jobs no longer carry — then survivor display + display-alias insert + rewrite `jobs.company` to display_name + delete loser rows); then **always** `merge_duplicate_positions` on its own connections; then `prune_empty_companies` for the deleted loser ids (no-op if already gone). No checkbox / opt-out. Raises `ValueError` (API 400) when `display_name`’s alias key is owned by a company **not** in `company_ids` (“already used by … — merge that company too”); does not auto-include third parties.
- `rename_company(db_path, company_id, display_name)` — update display_name, rewrite member jobs, ensure the new display is an alias (aliases still resolve). Raises `ValueError` (API 400) when the new display’s alias key belongs to a different company (“already used by … — merge that company instead”); rewrite uses only this company’s alias keys after insert.
- `delete_company(db_path, company_id)` — CASCADE-style delete of company + aliases and membership jobs; idempotent `{ already_gone: true }` when missing.
- `delete_companies(db_path, company_ids)` — one-txn batch via `delete_company_rows`; cascade deletes company + aliases + membership jobs per id; returns `{ company_ids, deleted_ids, already_gone_ids, jobs_deleted, count }` (`count` = `len(deleted_ids)`); missing ids are soft (`already_gone_ids`), not errors.

**Locked out:** hide, per-card edit, ML auto-merge, `jobs.company_id` FK, auto-create on upsert, CLI, `company_feedback` changes, rescoring as part of merge.

**Concurrency:** sync FastAPI handlers; catalog+job rewrite in one writer txn; dedupe uses separate connections afterward.

**Ambiguity picks (locked):**
- SSR Companies tab (not live JSON); blank companies never catalogued; company dashboard has no Companies tab.
- Company page chrome: stacked heading `{display} Positions` + `Total in DB: N` + optional expandable **Aliases (N)** as a real `<h2>` inside `<details>`/`<summary>` with a ▸/▼ expand marker (SSR from `get_company_alias_raws`, display-name key-matches dropped; omit when N=0). Tab-row `.controls-actions`: catalogued pages show text **Rename · Delete · Back**; uncatalogued show **Back** alone. Rename/Delete reuse the same prompt/confirm wording and `POST /api/company/rename` / `/delete` payloads as the Companies tab, but navigate differently — rename → `/company.html?company=<newDisplayName>` (no rebuild wait); delete → `/report.html` (no `?tab=companies`). Back is a `file://`-safe button via `apiUrl('/report.html')`. JS lives in `partials/dashboard_company_page_actions.js` (not `dashboard_companies.js`).
- Applied-company bonus keys = alias keys for companies with the Promote bit (`get_promoted_company_keys`). Apply / Interview-true auto-Promote when untouched; Unapply clears when untouched and idle; Stop dialog (client) only when Promote is on; manual checkbox sets touched.
- Merge: identity+job rewrite txn (Promote = OR; Avoid = OR; touched if either touched or Promote disagreed), then `merge_duplicate_positions` on separate connections, then prune empty losers.
- Company **Avoid** flag: owned outcome above — independent of Promote; early viewed only (no soft-hide, no Avoid score penalty, no parallel Avoid skip path); toggle shows Yes / No / Cancel bulk-viewed dialog; Off+Yes unviews all safe non-pipeline viewed membership jobs without clearing Promote/Touched; merge Avoid = OR.
